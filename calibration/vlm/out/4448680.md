# Mass-manufacturing and quality assurance solutions in SOFC value chain in FCH JU projects qSOFC and INNO-SOFC

<table>
  <tr>
    <th>Cells</th>
    <td>
      <strong>Targets</strong><br>
      • Reduce cell manufacturing cost to 400 €/kW at production volume of 10 MW/year<br>
      • Increase production yield in all parts of stack manufacturing value chain to above 95% by automation and quality assurance<br><br>
      <strong>Methods</strong><br>
      • Automated machine vision inspection system to detect faults during cell manufacturing<br>
      • Optimization of the cell manufacturing for high-speed manufacturing<br><br>
      <strong>Results</strong><br>
      • Higher and more homogeneous cell quality<br>
      • Higher yield and lower scrap rate throughout production
    </td>

    <th>Interconnects</th>
    <td>
      <strong>Targets</strong><br>
      • Interconnect manufacturing yield of 95%<br>
      • Development and validation of relevant quality assurance procedures in the interconnect manufacturing process<br><br>
      <strong>Methods</strong><br>
      • Failure mode and effect analysis (FMEA) of interconnect manufacturing process<br>
      • Automated laser-scanning of interconnect geometries<br>
      • Integrated leak tightness measurement system to check interconnect welds/brazes<br><br>
      <strong>Results</strong><br>
      • Improved quality of interconnects<br>
      • Reduced scrap rate
    </td>

    <th>Stacks</th>
    <td>
      <strong>Targets</strong><br>
      • Streamline stack manufacturing process and cut cost by increasing yield and lowering OPEX and CAPEX related to manufacturing infrastructure<br>
      • Manufacture stacks for 60 kW INNO-SOFC system<br><br>
      <strong>Methods</strong><br>
      • Testing and analysis of three different manufacturing and conditioning procedures using 30 stacks<br>
      • Statistical analysis of data<br><br>
      <strong>Results</strong><br>
      • Optimized stack assembly and conditioning processes identified<br>
      • Potential cost-reductions corresponding to each process identified
