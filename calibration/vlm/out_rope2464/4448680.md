# Mass-manufacturing and quality assurance solutions in SOFC value chain in FCH JU projects qSOFC and INNO-SOFC

<table>
  <tr>
    <td rowspan="2" style="background-color: #cce5ff; padding: 10px; vertical-align: top;">
      <h3>Cells</h3>
      <div style="margin-top: 20px;">
        ![image](image_5.png)
      </div>
      <div style="margin-top: 20px;">
        ![image](image_6.png)
      </div>
    </td>
    <td>
      <strong>Targets</strong>
      <ul>
        <li>Reduce cell manufacturing cost to 400 €/kW at production volume of 10 MW/year</li>
        <li>Increase production yield in all parts of stack manufacturing value chain to above 95% by automation and quality assurance</li>
      </ul>
      <strong>Methods</strong>
      <ul>
        <li>Automated machine vision inspection system to detect faults during cell manufacturing</li>
        <li>Optimization of the cell manufacturing for high-speed manufacturing</li>
      </ul>
      <strong>Results</strong>
      <ul>
        <li>Higher and more homogeneous cell quality</li>
        <li>Higher yield and lower scrap rate throughout production</li>
      </ul>
    </td>

    <td>
      <strong>Targets</strong>
      <ul>
        <li>Interconnect manufacturing yield of 95%</li>
        <li>Development and validation of relevant quality assurance procedures in the interconnect manufacturing process</li>
      </ul>
      <strong>Methods</strong>
      <ul>
        <li>Failure mode and effect analysis (FMEA) of interconnect manufacturing process</li>
        <li>Automated laser-scanning of interconnect geometries</li>
        <li>Integrated leak tightness measurement system to check interconnect welds/brazes</li>
      </ul>
      <strong>Results</strong>
      <ul>
        <li>Improved quality of interconnects</li>
        <li>Reduced scrap rate</li>
      </ul>

      <h3>Stacks</h3>
      <div style="margin-top: 20px;">
        ![image](image_14.png)
      </div>
    </td>
    <td>
      <strong>Targets</strong>
      <ul>
        <li>Streamline stack manufacturing process and cut cost by increasing yield and lowering OPEX and CAPEX related to manufacturing infrastructure</li>
        <li>Manufacture stacks for 60 kW INNO-SOFC system</li>
      </ul>
      <strong>Methods</strong>
      <ul>
        <li>Testing and analysis of three different manufacturing and conditioning procedures using 30 stacks</li>
        <li>Statistical analysis of data</li>
      </ul>
      <strong>Results</strong>
      <ul>
        <li>Optimized stack assembly and conditioning processes identified</li>
        <li>Potential cost-reductions corresponding to each process identified</li>
      </ul>
